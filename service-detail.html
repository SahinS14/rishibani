Overview

A private, unhurried reading of your Kundali & the match

Manglik Dosha is a Yoga in the Janma Kundali formed by Mars occupying the 1st, 4th, 7th, 8th or 12th house. In match making, this is read alongside the classical Guna Milan — a 36-point compatibility score across eight kootas — to see how two lives will actually meet.

Your acharya walks through the chart with you, explains the strength of each dosha, and offers remedies drawn from the classical texts — never fear-based, always practical. Predictions cover child prospects, family harmony, career support and the long-term direction of the relationship.

FAQ

Common questions

Do we both need to be present for the session?

It is ideal but not required. The reading can be delivered to one partner, and a follow-up shared with the other.

What if the exact birth time is unknown?

We can perform a birth-time rectification using major life events, or fall back to a Hora + Palmistry reading — please tell us at booking.

Are the remedies expensive or difficult?

No. Our remedies are simple mantra, daan and lifestyle practices from the classical texts — never fear-based or costly.

Is my information kept private?

Yes. All charts, notes and reports are strictly confidential to you and your acharya.
